# Creative image prompt — reference examples

These are full prompts you can paste into **Settings → Creative image prompt** and adapt.  
DALL·E in TrendWatcher outputs **1024×1024**; you can still describe square or vertical *composition* for a 1:1 crop.

---

## Example 1 — Minimal spiritual / astrology (square feel)

```
Create a minimalist spiritual advertisement design, square 1080x1080.
Background: soft cosmic gradient sky with subtle stars.
At the bottom of the image a small human silhouette stands looking up at the sky.
Above them a thin golden constellation path slowly forming across the sky.
The constellation lines look like a path leading forward.
Lighting: soft moonlight and warm golden starlight.
Atmosphere: calm cosmic night with subtle stardust.
Composition:
 large empty space above for text
 person small at the bottom
 very cinematic but minimal
Style:
 premium astrology brand
 modern spiritual design
 clean and elegant
```

**Copy ideas for overlays (not rendered by DALL·E in-app):**  
Headline: *You weren’t late.* · Subheadline: *You just didn’t know your timing yet.* · CTA: *Discover Your Timeline*

## Example 2 — Vertical lifestyle with text on clothing (9:16 feel)

```
Create a vertical social media advertisement image, 1080x1920 resolution.

STYLE:
Photorealistic lifestyle photography.
Natural lighting.
Feels like a real candid moment, not a staged advertisement.

SCENE:
A close-up shot of a person's arm wearing a simple white t-shirt.

Printed on the sleeve of the t-shirt is a block of text, like a message or announcement.

The camera focuses on the sleeve text while the rest of the person and background are slightly blurred.

ENVIRONMENT:
Warm indoor lighting, casual everyday environment.

MOOD:
Curious.
Unexpected.
Feels like you noticed a strange message written on someone's clothing.

COMPOSITION:
Vertical 9:16.
Text area clearly visible on the sleeve.

TEXT ON THE SLEEVE:

WE ARE LOOKING
FOR PEOPLE

WHO SOMETIMES
FEEL LIKE
THEY HAVE
NO ONE
TO TALK TO.

CTA (smaller line at bottom of sleeve):

YOU'RE NOT
THE ONLY ONE.
```

**Note:** DALL·E may distort or skip small text; treat on-image copy as experimental.
